Momo Night at Aarav's Flat

Aarav is cooking momo for his friends from TSI.

Shopping list

  • Flour (1 kg)
  • Minced chicken (500 g)
  • Onions and cabbage

Spice box

  • Ginger
  • Cumin
  • Garlic

Cooking steps

  1. Mix the flour with water and make a soft dough.
  2. Mix the chicken, onion and spices.
  3. Roll small circles of dough and fill them.
  4. Steam the momo for 12 minutes.

Kitchen words

Achar
A spicy tomato sauce served with momo.
Jhol
A warm, thin soup poured over momo.
