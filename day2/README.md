## How every bug-hunt works

1. Open the exercise's `index.html` with Live Server.
2. Read its `README.md`: it tells you how many bugs there are ("There are 5 bugs. Find at least 3.") and gives one hint per bug type.
3. Use your tools: your **eyes**, **HTMLHint** (Problems panel, Ctrl+Shift+M), **F12 → Elements / Styles**, **Lighthouse**.
4. Mend the file. Save. Check again.
5. Write your scoreboard line. Then the instructor shows the answers on the projector (the reveal).
