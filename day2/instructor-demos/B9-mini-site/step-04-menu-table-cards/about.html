About Didi

Didi grew up in Solukhumbu, where her mother made momos for the whole village. In 2015 she opened Momo House in Dillibazar with one steamer and ten plates.

Today she feeds students, office workers and tourists. Her rule is simple: soft dough, juicy filling, spicy achar.

"Cook with love, and the momo will fold itself." (Didi)
