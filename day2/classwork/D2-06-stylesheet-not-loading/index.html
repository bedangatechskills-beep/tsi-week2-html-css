Meet Didi

The cook behind Momo House

Her story

Didi grew up in Solukhumbu, where her mother made momos for the whole village. In 2015 she moved to Kathmandu and opened Momo House in Dillibazar with one steamer and ten plates. She still folds every momo by hand.

Her three rules

Soft dough. Juicy filling. Spicy achar. If one is missing, it is not a Momo House momo.

Momo House · Dillibazar, Kathmandu · Open 10 am to 9 pm
