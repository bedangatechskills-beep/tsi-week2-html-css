Before you book

Every trek starts and ends at our office in Thamel. We pick you up from your hotel in Kathmandu.

You need a TIMS card and a national park permit. We get them for you. Bring 4 passport photos.

Pack warm clothes. Nights in the mountains are cold, even in spring.

What is included

  • A licensed local guide
  • Tea-house rooms and three meals a day
  • Bus or jeep to the start of the trek
