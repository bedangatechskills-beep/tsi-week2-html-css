Kathmandu Ghost Walk

A night tour of old Kathmandu, by the TSI Story Club

The lady of Ason

Shopkeepers say a lady in a red shawl buys one candle every night, just before the shutters come down. Nobody has ever seen her leave.

The bell at midnight

In one courtyard near Indra Chowk, a small bell rings at midnight. There is no wind, and there is no bell in the courtyard.
