Didi's chicken momo

Makes 40 momo. Cooking time: 1 hour.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 500 g minced chicken
  • 1 onion
  • Spices
    • Ginger
    • Garlic
    • Cumin
    • Salt

Steps

  1. Mix the flour and water into a soft dough.
  2. Mix the chicken, onion and spices.
  3. Roll small circles and put a spoon of filling in each one.
  4. Steam for 12 minutes.

Kitchen words (bonus)

Achar
A spicy tomato sauce served with momo.
Steamer
A pot with layers. Steam from boiling water cooks the momo.
