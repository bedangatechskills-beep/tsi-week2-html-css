Our story

Our family has made clay pots near Patan Durbar Square for four generations. Every pot is shaped on a wheel that grandfather built, dried in the sun for three days and fired in the courtyard. We also sell singing bowls from Lalitpur metal workers and wooden windows carved by our neighbours.

Lunch break at the workshop

Every day at 1 pm, the potters stop for momos from the shop next door.
